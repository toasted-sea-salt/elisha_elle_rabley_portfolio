New York City
Ballet · Musical Theater
Joffrey Ballet School
Pre-Professional Ballet & Musical Theater Summer Intensives

One of America's most storied ballet schools, founded in New York in 1953 by Robert Joffrey. I trained in two of its New York summer intensives.

Pre-Professional Ballet Summer Intensive

Led by Artistic Director Era Jouravlev and Associate Artistic Director Stacy Caddell, the intensive is designed for advanced dancers preparing for a professional career in ballet. Through focused classical training, individualized artistic development, and mentorship from Joffrey Ballet School faculty, students refine their technique, deepen their performance quality, and build the discipline and versatility required for professional work. The curriculum may include Ballet Technique, Pointe, Ballet Repertoire, Pas de Deux, Contemporary Ballet, Kinetics, and Variations.

Musical Theater Intensive

Led by Artistic Directors Maximilien Baud and Matthew Prescott, the intensive brings the energy and versatility of Broadway into the studio. Through an immersive combination of dance, voice, acting, and performance, students develop the technique, confidence, stage presence, and storytelling of a well-rounded musical theater performer, learning from accomplished Broadway professionals and teaching artists in the heart of New York City.

Its invitation-only Pre-Professional Track is designed for advanced performers ages 14 and older pursuing more rigorous, career-focused training. Students are invited through their Summer Intensive acceptance and must enroll in a complete two-week session. The track adds advanced training, master workshops, private vocal instruction, personalized mentorship, and further career- and performance-focused opportunities led by Broadway professionals.

Vitacca Ballet & School for Dance
Accelerated Homeschool Division

Vitacca's audition-only track for homeschooled dancers combines a full academic course load with a professional-level training schedule. At more than 45 hours a week in the studio, it is one of the most demanding youth programs in Houston. The curriculum pairs advanced classical ballet with contemporary and modern technique. Vitacca was named a Youth America Grand Prix Outstanding School in 2025.

Classical Ballet Pointe Contemporary / Modern Full-Time Track

Houston Ballet Academy
Summer Intensive

Houston Ballet is one of the largest professional ballet companies in the United States, and its Academy is a major feeder into the company. The Summer Intensive admits students through a national audition tour. Accepted dancers spend several weeks in daily technique, pointe, variations, and contemporary classes taught by Academy faculty.

Houston, TX
Contemporary · Jazz · Modern
METdance
Pre-Professional Training

METdance, formerly Houston Metropolitan Dance Company, is a professional contemporary repertory company with its own training center. Its pre-professional program trains young dancers in ballet, pointe, jazz, modern, and contemporary to prepare them for university dance programs and professional careers. Alumni have gone on to schools such as Juilliard.

Contemporary Jazz Modern Ballet & Pointe
Houston, TX

Houston Institute for Contemporary Dance
HC2 · Houston Contemporary 2

HC2 is the pre-professional youth company of the Institute for Contemporary Dance and a sister company to the professional Houston Contemporary Dance Company. It is concert-based and non-competitive. Dancers train across multiple styles with professional choreographers and perform throughout Houston, including under the professional company's banner.

Why It Matters

What the studio taught me.

Discipline at Volume

Training 45+ hours a week while keeping up with school taught me to manage time hard, and to show up consistently whether or not I felt like it.

Coachability

Ballet is daily, precise correction. I learned to take feedback without ego and apply it right away. That's the same loop I use in research and product work.

Performing Under Pressure

Auditions, belt tests, and live performance all give you one shot. I'm comfortable being evaluated in the moment, whether that's a stage, a case interview, or a pitch.
